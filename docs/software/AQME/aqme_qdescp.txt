AQME-QDESCP module help

Native backend: AQME QDESCP
Purpose: descriptor and feature extraction.

Inputs:
- CSV containing SMILES and code_name columns.
- Folder containing SDF or XYZ structures.

The GUI exposes the input, program, optimization backend, charge/multiplicity,
descriptor targets, solvent, temperature, and geometry optimization toggle.
Other native AQME flags should be entered in Extra options / flags.
